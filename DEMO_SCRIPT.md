# SWOT Challenger: the five-minute demo

Version 1.0.0. The English script first, then the same in German.

## English

**For** a managing director, a head of strategy or a business-unit lead. **Goal:** after five minutes they can repeat one sentence: *"AI agents found what Arvento's SWOT gets wrong or leaves out, €4.9m a year at stake on 5 of 6 issues; two strategies lift its response to the market from 1.60 to 2.55, past the average, and code computed every figure."*

### The four figures at the top

| Figure | What it shows | In the demo |
|---|---|---|
| At stake a year | The expected cost of the issues the challenge priced, if nothing changes | €4.9m on 5 of 6 issues, then €3.3m, with an upside of €700k a year |
| Items challenged | How many items of the SWOT the challenge questions | 6 of 14, then 2 of them addressed now |
| Response to the market | The EFE score of Fred R. David's external factor evaluation; 2.50 is average | 1.60, then 2.55 |
| Session cost | What a live run of this company costs | < €0.01, estimated, simulated run (the guide adds €0.08 on Thorough) |

### Before the call

1. Chrome or Edge, full screen (F11), zoom at 100 %; other tabs closed, notifications off.
2. Walk the five steps once. The simulation needs no key.
3. Let the guide open, or start at `?guide=0` if you explain it yourself. `?scenario=s2&step=4` opens a step; `?lang=de` opens in German, and EN | DE switches without losing the step.
4. For a live run, bring your own Claude API key from a workspace with a spend limit. Balanced costs less than a cent.

### The five minutes

The guide's last chapter, *Show it in five minutes*, with Arvento Drives. Play walks the run and stops wherever you act, at **Your turn**. Space pauses; → or a clicker moves on, ← goes back.

1. **Point at the key figures.** (1 min)
   "Before anything runs, the case is on the table: €4.9m on 5 of 6 issues at stake a year."
2. **Press Start the agents.** (1 min)
   "The team wrote this SWOT. Lilac marks what the AI added."
3. **Press Challenge this SWOT.** (1 min)
   "Red marks 6 of 14 items, and a threat nobody listed joins the SWOT."
4. **Keep the two strategies ticked and press Adopt these strategies.** (1 min)
   "The response to the market rises from 1.60 to 2.55, past the average, and €3.3m a year is left at stake."
5. **Read the summary, then press Download the report.** (1 min)
   "The AI wrote the words; every figure in them comes from the code. The report takes it all with you, with the footer on every page."

### Questions you will get

**Is Arvento real?** No. The three companies and their figures are fictional; the facts around them are real, with sources dated in DATA_SOURCES.md.

**Where do the euros come from?** The Analyst gives each issue a euro range a year, in the sector's own KPIs. Code takes the midpoint times the likelihood and adds them up. It is a structured judgement, not a measurement, and the page says so.

**What does the AI decide?** Nothing. It completes, challenges and estimates; the person checks its items and chooses the strategies, and code computes every score.

**Which methods?** The factor tables and the IE matrix follow Fred R. David's Strategic Management, the TOWS matrix Heinz Weihrich (1982). The Method button explains each.

**Can it do our company?** Yes: Run it live, with your own key and public information only. Nothing is kept; the key lives in the tab's memory.

### If something goes wrong

- **The page stays on its loading screen.** It was opened from a file; use the published address or serve the folder over http.
- **A live run stops early.** The right-hand column says why and what to do next; Play a simulation goes on with the demo.
- **The page moved on while you talked.** Press ← to go back; Space pauses.
- **The PDF has no page numbers.** Firefox and Safari leave them out; save the PDF from Chrome or Edge.

### Fragen, die kommen

**Gibt es Arvento?** Nein. Die drei Unternehmen und ihre Zahlen sind erfunden, die Fakten um sie herum echt, mit Quellen und Datum in DATA_SOURCES.md.

**Woher kommen die Euro?** Der Analyst gibt jedem Problem eine Euro-Spanne pro Jahr, in den Kennzahlen der Branche. Code nimmt die Mitte mal die Wahrscheinlichkeit und zählt zusammen. Das ist ein strukturiertes Urteil, keine Messung, und die Seite sagt es.

**Was entscheidet die KI?** Nichts. Sie ergänzt, prüft und schätzt; der Mensch prüft ihre Punkte und wählt die Strategien, und Code rechnet jeden Wert.

**Welche Methoden?** Die Faktortabellen und die IE-Matrix folgen Fred R. Davids Strategic Management, die TOWS-Matrix Heinz Weihrich (1982). Die Schaltfläche Methode erklärt jede.

**Geht das mit unserem Unternehmen?** Ja: Live ausführen, mit eigenem Schlüssel und nur öffentlichen Informationen. Nichts wird gespeichert; der Schlüssel bleibt im Speicher des Tabs.
