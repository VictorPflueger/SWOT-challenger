# SWOT Challenger

Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats, put to the test.

A browser tool, still at the planning stage. A person names a company and enters what they know of its SWOT analysis. AI agents complete it, challenge every item, put a figure on what is wrong or missing and turn it into strategies. The result downloads as a report.

Nothing here runs yet. [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) is the plan that Victor approves before the first milestone.

- **Simulation** will play three fictional companies, with no key and nothing sent anywhere.
- **Live** will run the agents on your company with your own Claude API key, from your browser to Anthropic's API and nowhere else. Nothing is kept: close the tab and it is gone.

Public information only: never enter confidential, personal or otherwise protected data.

It will be published at https://victorpflueger.github.io/SWOT-challenger/, as one of four tools built as a set: the Business Model Canvas, SWOT and PESTEL challengers and the Prototype Ideator, which turns their reports into prototype ideas.
